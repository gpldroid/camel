Welcome to Camel Blog. This publication is being built as a home for practical articles, clear explanations and useful ideas.

## What to expect

Articles will be organised around focused topics and written to answer real questions. Each guide should explain its subject clearly, distinguish established information from opinion, and provide useful next steps where appropriate.

## How the site is maintained

The site is generated from files stored in GitHub. Editors can use the content manager at **/admin/** after the GitHub OAuth connection has been configured. Drafts and published articles are stored as Markdown files, making the content portable and easy to back up.

## Editorial standard

We aim to keep articles accurate, readable and up to date. If you notice an error or have a suggestion, please use the Contact page once its public email address has been added.

Thank you for reading.
