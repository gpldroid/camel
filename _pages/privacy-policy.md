This Privacy Policy explains how Camel Blog may handle information when you visit this website.

The hosting provider may process technical data such as IP address, browser type, requested pages and timestamps for security, delivery and diagnostics.

If an analytics service is enabled, it may use cookies or similar technologies to measure site usage. Analytics is disabled until a valid ID is configured. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

## Advertising
Advertising may be added through Google AdSense after the publisher configures a valid account and ID. Third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, subject to their own policies and applicable consent requirements.

## External links
This site may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.
